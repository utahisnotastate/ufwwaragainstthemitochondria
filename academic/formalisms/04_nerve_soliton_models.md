# 4. Nerve Pulses: Hodgkin–Huxley vs Electromechanical Models

**Evidence tier:** Hodgkin–Huxley ionic mechanism — **A**. Heimburg–Jackson membrane soliton hypothesis — **B** (published, contested). “Phase-locked ultrasonic soliton + biophoton Wi-Fi cognition packets” — **X**.

## Scientific question

Is the action potential adequately described by voltage-gated ionic currents alone, or do electromechanical membrane degrees of freedom play a necessary role?

## Accepted baseline (Tier A)

The Hodgkin–Huxley (HH) framework and modern channel biophysics explain electrical excitability across neuroscience and medicine. Local anesthetics, channelopathies, and patch-clamp data are interpreted in this language.

## Electromechanical soliton hypothesis (Tier B)

Heimburg & Jackson (*PNAS*, 2005) proposed that near lipid melting transitions, nonlinear compressibility enables density-pulse (soliton) propagation in membranes, potentially accounting for reversible heat, mechanical swelling, and related non-electrical observations:

\[
\frac{\partial^2 \Delta\rho}{\partial t^2}
=
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\!\left[
\bigl(c_0^2 + p\,\Delta\rho + q\,(\Delta\rho)^2\bigr)
\frac{\partial \Delta\rho}{\partial x}
\right]
- h\,\frac{\partial^4 \Delta\rho}{\partial x^4}
\]

Reviews and debates continue; proposed empirical tests emphasize the unresolved question of whether neuronal membranes actually traverse the required phase transition during spikes ([arXiv:1808.07193](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.07193)).

Ultra-weak photon emission (UPE) from living tissue is a reported phenomenon in biophysics, but it is **not** established as a microtubule optical backbone for “cognitive packets” or Fröhlich condensation as an engineering transceiver.

## Rejected from source batch

- Replacing HH with solitons as settled fact.
- FPGA “40 Hz Gamma” ultrasonic phase-lock as a neuromorphic product that synchronizes biophoton lattices.
- OPM-MEG + Stratix FPGA + 1.6 MHz phased arrays presented as production “soliton transducers” for cognition.

## Open problems

- Decisive experiments distinguishing HH-compatible mechanical side-effects from necessary soliton dynamics.
- Safe, evidence-based neuromodulation (TMS, tDCS, focused ultrasound) already exists as a medical field — keep claims inside that literature.
