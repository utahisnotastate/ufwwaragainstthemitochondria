# 7. Pathogen Mechanics (Literature Summary Only)

**Evidence tier:** Elastic / acoustic modeling of viral capsids — **B**. Ultrasound effects on enveloped viruses in controlled experiments — **B**. In-vivo “resonant lysis + cathodic terrain clamp” as a deployable medical system — **X** (stripped; dual-use and unsupported as clinical engineering).

> **Integrity note:** This file intentionally contains **no** build instructions, power levels for tissue application, electrode layouts, or pathogen-targeting protocols. Those were removed from the source batch.

## Scientific question

Can mechanical resonance of viral particles be modeled and, in vitro or for disinfection, exploited without harming host cells?

## Accepted baseline (Tier A)

- Antiviral medicine is grounded in antivirals, vaccines, monoclonal antibodies, public-health controls, and hospital infection control.
- Mammalian cells and virions differ in size and mechanics; selective acoustic effects are an active research hypothesis, not a finished therapy.

## Modeling literature (Tier B)

Elastic-shell / continuum models estimate vibrational eigenvalues of capsids (Lamb-type modes). Example schematic form:

\[
\omega_{l,n}
=
\frac{\xi_{l,n}}{R}
\sqrt{\frac{E}{\rho(1+\sigma)}}
\]

Atomistic / coarse-grained calculations (e.g., Dykeman & Sankey) predict low-frequency modes relevant to proposed “mechanodestruction” ideas. Geometry and environment shift frequencies substantially, so single-frequency magic numbers are unreliable.

Experimental and theoretical work explores ultrasound ranges for envelope disruption / inactivation in **laboratory or disinfection contexts** (see e.g. discussions in *Scientific Reports* and *Appl. Phys. Lett.*-class literature on resonance matching). Translating that into safe human therapy is a separate, heavily regulated problem.

## Terrain theory / voltage “cure” claims

Electrochemical potentials of tissues matter in wound healing and electrophysiology research. They do **not** validate Bechamp-style “terrain” narratives that replace germ theory, nor do they support clamping extracellular potential to a slogan millivoltage as a universal antimicrobial.

## Rejected from source batch

- Combined GHz DRA + gold cathodic gel-pad “ECM bias head.”
- Claims that opportunistic pathogens “revert to commensal cycles” under \(-70\,\mathrm{mV}\) clamps.
- Any production schematic for selective in-vivo lysis.

## Open problems (research community)

- Robust frequency prediction across mutant geometries and media.
- Distinguishing cavitation, heating, and true resonant mechanical failure.
- Therapeutic windows (if any) that spare host tissue — only via regulated biomedical research.
