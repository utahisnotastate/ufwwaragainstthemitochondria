# 1. Interfacial Water & Prebiotic Chemistry Limits

**Evidence tier:** Exclusion of colloids near hydrophilic surfaces — **B**. Ordered “fourth-phase” \(H_3O_2\) water as metabolic battery — **C / X**. Spontaneous enzyme-free abiogenesis in IR-pumped nano-cavities — **X**.

## Scientific question

What is the physical nature of water layers near hydrophilic interfaces, and can interfacial fields assist prebiotic chemistry?

## Accepted baseline (Tier A)

- Liquid water’s structure and dynamics are described by hydrogen-bond networks, dielectric response, and molecular dynamics — not a stable macroscopic crystalline “fourth phase.”
- Life’s known biochemistry depends on enzymes, templates (RNA/DNA), and compartmentalization; abiogenesis remains an open research field with many competing models (surface catalysis, wet–dry cycling, hydrothermal vents, etc.).

## What is empirically supported (Tier B)

**Exclusion zones (EZ):** Near some hydrophilic surfaces (e.g., Nafion), colloidal microspheres are excluded over tens to hundreds of micrometers. Multiple groups have reproduced the *exclusion* phenomenology.

**Competing explanation:** Diffusiophoresis / chemotaxis in ion and pH gradients (Schurr and others) can explain exclusion, long-range potentials, and EZ growth kinetics without requiring long-range ordered water. Critical reviews conclude Pollack’s structural theory is not established ([MDPI IJMS review](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/21/14/5041); Schurr, *J. Phys. Chem. B*).

**Infrared absorption features** near ~270 nm and birefringence claims remain contested and are not a license to treat EZ water as \(H_3O_2\) capacitor plates powering biology in place of oxidative phosphorylation.

## Useful (conservative) interfacial electrostatics

Near a charged interface, the equilibrium ion distribution is described by Poisson–Boltzmann (or Poisson–Nernst–Planck) physics:

\[
\nabla \cdot \bigl(\varepsilon \nabla \phi\bigr) = -\rho_f - \sum_k z_k e\, c_k(\phi)
\]

with Boltzmann (or Nernst–Planck) closure for concentrations \(c_k\). This is standard electrochemistry — not QED “coherence domain” engineering.

## Rejected from source batch (Tier X)

- Del Giudice–Preparata Hamiltonian presented as a *protocol* that collapses water into coherence domains of radius \(\approx 0.1\,\mu\mathrm{m}\) that seed life.
- Claims that \(\dot{S}_\mathrm{gen}=0\) at cavity walls via impedance matching to \(Z_0 \approx 376.73\,\Omega\).
- “Prebiotic cavity matrix” hardware that polymerizes formamide/glycine without enzymes by IR pumping.
- Treating ATP as merely “protein unfolding to expose water” while the environment is the true fuel — conflicts with extensively validated chemiosmotic / OXPHOS biochemistry.

## Open problems

- Quantify when exclusion is chemotactic vs any residual interfacial ordering.
- Map which interfacial chemistries (minerals, wet–dry cycles) are experimentally productive for prebiotic oligomers — without inventing cavity “engines.”
