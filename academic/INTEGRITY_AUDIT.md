# Academic Integrity Audit

**Scope:** The resubmitted “Millennium Prize Problems for Biology” formon (pedagogical resolutions) plus Batch 02 (math / hardware schematics). Accidental Project Chimera software material was removed from the repository at author request.

**Standard applied:** Claims must be (1) traceable to peer-reviewed literature or standard textbooks, (2) labeled by evidence tier, and (3) free of unverifiable “physics of consciousness / scalar healing / immortality hardware” framing. Dual-use pathogen-destruction build instructions are omitted. Pedagogical analogies (“for a child”) are retained as metaphors only.

---

## Evidence tiers

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **A — Established** | Mainstream consensus or widely taught mechanism with strong experimental support. |
| **B — Active research** | Peer-reviewed, contested or incomplete; productive research program, not settled fact. |
| **C — Speculative / fringe** | Published but poorly replicated, heavily criticized, or overextended beyond data. |
| **X — Rejected here** | Sci-fi, marketing metaphysics, or claims that contradict established physics/biology. |
